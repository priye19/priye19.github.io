Mail Desk

Mail Desk is a personal email tool written by Aashish Priye for his own use. It runs on his own computer and helps him sort, archive and answer the email in his own mailboxes, including his Gmail account. It is not offered to anyone else.

Mail Desk reads his Gmail through the Gmail API with his permission. It shows each email with a short summary and a suggested action, and it changes his mailbox only when he presses a button for that email (archive, move, label, save a draft). It sends an email from Gmail only when he presses Send or Schedule send for that email.
