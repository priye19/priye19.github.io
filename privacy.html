Mail Desk privacy policy

Mail Desk is a personal tool used only by Aashish Priye, on his own Gmail account (priye.tamu@gmail.com). No other person's account is connected to it.

What it reads

With his permission, Mail Desk reads the messages, labels, filters and settings of his Gmail account through the Gmail API, so that it can show and sort his email.

Where the data is kept

The email it reads is stored only in a database on his own computer. The Google access key is stored only on that computer. Nothing is stored on any public server.

Who it is shared with

Mail Desk does not sell, share or publish any Gmail data. To write summaries and draft replies, it passes the text of an email to Anthropic's Claude service under his own account, which handles it under Anthropic's terms for his account. No Gmail data is used for advertising.

What it changes

Mail Desk changes his mailbox only when he presses a button for a particular email: archive, move to a label, move to Trash, save a draft, or create a filter. It never sends email from Gmail.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Mail Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
